Plumbing FAQ

Common questions

Do you do emergency call-outs?

Yes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For burst pipes, major leaks or no water, call 07481 105780 any time.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. Leaking taps, running toilets and blocked sinks are a big part of what we do.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We look at the problem, tell you what's wrong and agree the price with you before any work begins.

Can you work on unvented hot water cylinders?

Yes. We're G3 qualified, so we're certified to install, service and repair unvented cylinders.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Turn off the water at your stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink), switch off electrics near the leak, then call us on 07481 105780. We answer emergencies 24/7.

How it works

Six steps to your new bathroom

Here's what to expect from the first visit to the day you use your new bathroom.

  1. Free survey

    We visit, measure up, check the existing pipework and talk through what you want.

  2. Bespoke design & quote

    We design the layout around how you use the room and the look you want, then give you a fixed written quote. Choose your suite and tiles in your own time.

  3. Strip-out

    We protect your home and remove the old bathroom. We can arrange disposal of the old suite and waste for you.

  4. First fix

    New pipework, waste and any wall or floor preparation, done before anything is closed up.

  5. Panelling, tiling & finishing

    Wall panels or tiles fitted, flooring laid, boxing-in finished and everything sealed.

  6. Second fix & handover

    Suite connected, everything tested for leaks, the room cleaned and the work explained to you.

Bathroom FAQ

Common questions

How much does a new bathroom cost?

It depends on the size of the room, the suite you choose and how much the layout changes. After a free survey we give you a fixed written price, so you know the full cost before you commit.

What affects the price of a new bathroom?

The main things are the size of the room, the suite and tiles you choose, whether the layout changes (moving a toilet or shower means new pipework and waste), and extras like underfloor heating or a wet room. We talk you through each of these at the free survey so there are no surprises.

Can I supply my own suite and tiles?

Yes. We're happy to fit items you've bought yourself, or we can recommend suppliers and order everything for you.

Will I be without a toilet during the work?

If it's your only toilet, we plan the work so you're never without one overnight.
